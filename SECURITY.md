# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for `ckir/rsutils`: open the
repository's Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability".

If that is not available to you, contact TODO-CONTACT.

## What to include

- The affected tool or library and its version (or the commit).
- Your operating system.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The impact as you understand it.

## Response

This is a small project maintained in spare time. There is no service-level
agreement: reports are read and answered on a best-effort basis.
